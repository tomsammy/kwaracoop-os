# Kwara State Executive Council Memorandum (SEC MEMO)

**MEMORANDUM BY THE HONOURABLE COMMISSIONER FOR BUSINESS, INNOVATION AND TECHNOLOGY**

---

### SUBJECT: 
**APPROVAL FOR THE DEPLOYMENT OF THE KWARA STATE COOPERATIVE MANAGEMENT AND REGULATORY INFORMATION SYSTEM (“KWARA-COOP OS”) ACROSS THE 16 LOCAL GOVERNMENT AREAS**

---

## 1. PURPOSE
The purpose of this Memorandum is to seek the approval of the Kwara State Executive Council, chaired by His Excellency, the Executive Governor of Kwara State, for the adoption, statewide deployment, and institutionalization of **KwaraCoop OS**—a state-wide digital cooperative banking, regulatory oversight, and credit risk ecosystem.

---

## 2. BACKGROUND & CURRENT CHALLENGES
Kwara State possesses one of the most vibrant grassroots cooperative ecosystems in North-Central Nigeria, comprising over **1,480 registered cooperative societies** and an estimated **₦16.8 Billion** in circulating community capital across the three Senatorial Districts (Kwara Central, South, and North).

However, the existing operational model suffers from significant vulnerabilities:
1. **Pervasive Bookkeeping Opacity:** Manual paper ledgers create opportunities for executive embezzlement and accounting reconciliation disputes.
2. **"Loan Stacking" & Credit Defaults:** Unscrupulous borrowers exploit regulatory blindspots to secure simultaneous loans from multiple societies across different LGAs (e.g. borrowing in Ilorin West and defaulting while borrowing again in Offa or Asa), threatening the liquidity of primary societies.
3. **Sub-optimal Government Subsidy Distribution:** Government agricultural interventions (NPK fertilizers, certified seeds, tractor access vouchers) and **KWASSIP** micro-credits encounter leakages due to unverified cooperative membership lists.
4. **Uncollected Statutory Revenue:** Cooperative societies frequently default on mandatory annual registration renewal fees payable to the **Kwara State Internal Revenue Service (KWIRS)** due to manual follow-up constraints.

---

## 3. THE PROPOSED SOLUTION: KWARA-COOP OS

KwaraCoop OS introduces a dual-tier architecture:

### A. For the State Government (Regulatory Tier)
* **The Centralized Credit Risk Registry:** An automated registry indexed by National Identity Number (NIN) and BVN, preventing loan stacking across the 16 LGAs.
* **Automated KWIRS IGR Integration:** Mandatory digital annual e-licensing and audit submissions linked directly to the KWIRS payment gateway, expanding the state’s non-oil IGR.
* **Targeted Intervention Verification:** A verified digital pipeline for routing farm inputs from the Ministry of Agriculture and credit from KWASSIP directly to genuine, active farmers in agrarian clusters (Patigi, Edu, Kaiama, and Baruten).

### B. For Grassroots Members (Citizen Tier)
* **Dedicated NUBAN Bank Accounts:** Every cooperative member is issued a unique 10-digit virtual bank account number, enabling instant deposits from any commercial bank (GTB, FirstBank, OPay).
* **Multi-Channel Instant Alerts:** Members receive immediate SMS and push notifications for every salary deduction, *Ajo* collection, or loan transaction.
* **Rural USSD & Offline Availability:** Seamless offline collection using handheld thermal POS units and feature-phone USSD (`*384*24#`), ensuring full inclusion in remote areas with poor data connectivity.

## 5. PHASED ROLLOUT SCHEDULE (12 MONTHS)

| Phase | Duration | Scope of Work | Target LGAs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase I: Pilot** | Months 1 – 3 | Deployment of Ministry Regulatory Portal; digitization of top 100 civil service & market cooperatives. | Ilorin West, Ilorin East, Ilorin South |
| **Phase II: Commercial Hubs** | Months 4 – 6 | Rollout to commercial traders, artisans, and transport unions; activation of KWIRS payment rails. | Offa, Oyun, Irepodun, Ifelodun |
| **Phase III: Agrarian North** | Months 7 – 9 | Field POS & USSD offline rollout for rice, maize, and fish farmers; farm inputs distribution integration. | Patigi, Edu, Kaiama, Baruten, Moro, Asa |
| **Phase IV: Consolidation** | Months 10 – 12 | State-wide Credit Risk Registry enforcement; full integration with KWASSIP and national agricultural schemes. | All 16 LGAs |

---

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS / PRAYERS FOR COUNCIL
The State Executive Council is respectfully invited to:
1. **APPROVE** the adoption of **KwaraCoop OS** as the official, unified cooperative management and regulatory oversight platform for Kwara State.
2. **DIRECT** the Directorate of Cooperatives under the Ministry of Business, Innovation and Technology to enforce the digital enrollment of all 1,480+ societies within 12 calendar months.
3. **MANDATE** the integration of the Kwara State Internal Revenue Service (KWIRS) payment gateway for all statutory licensing and audit certification fees.
4. **AUTHORIZE** the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development and the Kwara State Social Investment Programmes (KWASSIP) to utilize the KwaraCoop verified registry for all future cooperative-targeted subsidy and credit disbursements.
